## 0. What was wrong

Every earlier ANWM verdict used **6.46 deg**, but that is the TII conformal threshold for the **full-parity** fit at
`k_d` 0.295. Amendment 8 makes **drag-free + lag-only** primary, and in that mode the TII threshold is **14.73 deg**.
Applying 6.46 to lag-only residuals over-counts infeasibility by design — most visibly the designed-10 family.

**Withdrawn:** "Regime C testable-and-infeasible is 11/11 (lag-only)". That count applied the parity-0.295 cut to
lag-only residuals. At its own mode's threshold the same residuals give 0/12 for the 10-deg cell and 10/10 for 20 deg.

## 4. Injection scoring, frozen

`configs/injection_preregistration.yaml` is the frozen record. Its numbers:

- **Corpus.** 46 injectable Regime C clips; 3 slalom-5 course clips are not reproducible and are excluded. Each is
  paired with its **existing level clip** (same start, seed, path) — no new level clips are generated.
- **Noise.** Track B's 8 regenerated pairs differ by 1-2.4 of 255 (0.4-0.9 %). The degree-equivalent repeat noise is
  measured from those pairs at Checkpoint 2; a cell whose repeat noise exceeds the released-frame p99 (4.20 deg) is
  reported but not counted.
- **Unit.** `(maneuver x designed tilt)` cells, not clips; bootstrap over starts. Prior coverage enters as a covariate
  (accel-20 0.148, climb-20 0.217), reported per cell.
- **Endpoint.** Slope of depicted on injected tilt in the injected arm, degrees per degree.
- **Verdicts, pre-registered.** *Following*: slope >= 0.50 (half the required slope of 1.0) **and** the 95 % CI
  excludes the level-arm slope 0.04. *Ignoring*: 95 % CI upper bound <= 0.15. *Partial*: anything else.
- **Controls.** A 15-clip mirrored-sign arm; if its slope does not invert, the arm is void.
- **Secondary only.** Projected priors (unfilled regions; the estimator is not validated on them).
- **Stop rule.** Ignoring -> stop (more level clips cannot help); partial -> diagnose the cell; following -> the
  144-clip design needs a new approval.

Nothing is scored under this amendment until Track B's Checkpoint 2 lands.
