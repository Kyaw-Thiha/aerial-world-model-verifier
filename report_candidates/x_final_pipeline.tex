\section{One path set, four generators}
\label{sec:x-generators}

The verifier's evidence base was one generator plus two pilots that failed their own roll-response
gate. To test whether a path-conditioned aerial world model depicts the attitude its commanded path
requires, we drove four released models on one common path set at their native frame grids:
ANWM (4-DoF actions at 1\,s), LingBot-World (camera poses per frame), SolarWM-5B (absolute camera
poses) and SANA-WM, added here because it is the only candidate that takes a metric camera
trajectory natively (\texttt{--camera}, $(F,4,4)$), releases Apache-2.0 weights without a gate, and
runs on one GPU with the release's own script. Models whose conditioning is a keyboard or mouse
action DSL (Matrix-Game, Yume) were not used: driving them from a metric path would be an
approximation, not a command.

\paragraph{The common path set}
Twenty-five paths in five classes --- level, mild lateral acceleration, banked turns of 10--25$^\circ$,
longitudinal (climb, descent, accelerate/brake), and three out-of-envelope demands --- plus two
real UZH-FPV windows and two scene swaps. A path is a family and a requested peak; each model
receives the same family at the same peak on its own time base, and the achieved peak of the
commanded attitude is solved to within 0.25$^\circ$. ANWM saturates on four high-bank paths because
its 45$^\circ$-per-step travel-direction cap cannot express a 25$^\circ$ bank at 2.8\,m\,s$^{-1}$;
those cells are flagged as not expressible and keep their achieved peak.

\paragraph{Tilt fidelity}
For each clip we fit, per axis, the depicted lean on the required lean with an intercept, over
in-envelope frames carrying at least 3$^\circ$ of commanded lean and 2$^\circ$ of excitation, and
report the mean slope as the \emph{tilt gain}: 1 is a generator that depicts the commanded attitude,
0 one that ignores it, negative one that mirrors it. The identity mapping recovers the commanded
lean to 0.0$^\circ$, so the statistic has no built-in bias. The frozen gain fitter cannot be used
here: its drag channel is collinear with the intercept when the commanded speed is constant, and
every clip of this set abstains on conditioning (condition numbers $1.7\times10^{5}$ to
$3.1\times10^{5}$ against a $10^{3}$ ceiling).

\paragraph{The final pipeline}
Only checks that passed their own pre-registered criteria enter: the lean-consistency check on a
lean propagated from a path anchor, with a drift-flag abstention and a hard abstention where no
anchor exists, window-level verdicts (12 frames, hop 4) and a clip-level Benjamini--Hochberg rule
at $q=0.10$. Body-rate consistency (P1), the thrust envelope (P2) and the vertical check (V0) are
reported as negatives: their own criteria failed or their stop rule fired. The pipeline's binding
quantity is coverage, not accuracy: the anchored start exists for 16 of 200 development clips and
for none of the forest tilt-removal clips, so the pipeline abstains far more often than the reader
start would --- a limitation that is pre-registered as a success criterion rather than discovered
after the fact.

\paragraph{What this does and does not establish}
It establishes a generator-level measurement with a null near zero, and a final pipeline assembled
only from checks that survived their own tests. It does not establish held-out behaviour: the frozen
held-out pass was not executed in this round, and the report records that rather than a number.
