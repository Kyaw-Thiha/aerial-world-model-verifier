\subsection{Fine-tuning the depicted-tilt reader}
\label{sec:g03-reader}

The detection floor, the abstention rate and the false-reject spread are set by the depicted-tilt
reader rather than by the physics. We fine-tune GeoCalib on \num{5007} mocap-labelled TII frames
with leave-one-flight-out folds: each frame's label is the world-up direction in the camera
frame, built from the truth attitude and the analytic mount, so no pixel estimate enters a label.
Scored against those labels before any fine-tuning, the frozen reader reproduces its published
error (median \SI{3.97}{deg}, \SI{90}{\percent}-trimmed RMSE \SI{4.81}{deg}, \SI{12.7}{\percent}
of frames above \SI{10}{deg}), which validates the label construction. Training runs GeoCalib's
own LM refinement inside the loop (the signal the authors train on), with a cosine loss on the up
direction, AdamW at \num{2e-5}, two epochs, 700 frames per fold and no geometric augmentation.

\paragraph{Result.} The pooled held-out median error falls \SI{28}{\percent} (Table~\ref{tab:g03-reader}),
which meets the pre-registered bar, and the trimmed RMSE falls \SI{23}{\percent}; the fraction of
frames above \SI{10}{deg} falls only from \SI{12.7}{\percent} to \SI{10.8}{\percent}, which does
not meet the second bar. The gains are where the frozen reader is worst in the tail: the piloted
track flight's trimmed RMSE halves (\SI{18.4}{} to \SI{9.9}{deg}), while the autonomous track
flight stays at \SI{31.5}{deg} because its worst frames are near-ceiling views that attitude
supervision does not repair.

\paragraph{Downstream.} Rebuilding each clip's depicted lean from the fine-tuned gravity, with the
path side, the statistics and the confidence gate frozen, the clip-level median residual falls on
six of eight flights (e.g.\ \SI{10.2}{} to \SI{7.7}{deg} on \texttt{01p}, \SI{16.3}{} to
\SI{13.7}{deg} on \texttt{13p}) and rises on two. With a leave-one-flight-out threshold
calibrated on the gate-kept clips, the new reader false-rejects 5 of 72 clips (\SI{6.9}{\percent})
and flags 55 of 72 (\SI{76.4}{\percent}) under a full tilt removal; the ungated threshold is
still \SI{18}{}-\SI{33}{deg}, i.e.\ the per-flight transfer problem of
Section~\ref{sec:g01-segments} is untouched. Abstention does not move at all: the same 18 of 100
real clips abstain with either reader under the rules that do not need a bootstrap, so a better
estimate buys accuracy, not admissibility.

\paragraph{Caveats.} Eight flights are the whole evidence base and each fold is a single flight.
The UZH-FPV forward rigs (three sequences, 1,097 pinhole frames, leave-one-sequence-out) are a
separate route with separate folds: the medians halve (1.08-1.48 to 0.61-0.74\,deg) but the tail
does not improve, so the transfer is a level effect, not a tail effect. The confidence-weighted-loss ablation declared in the pre-registration
was not run. The mount and drag ablations are a different, untouched axis.
