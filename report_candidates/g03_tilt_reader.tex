\subsection{Fine-tuning the depicted-tilt reader}
\label{sec:g03-reader}

The detection floor, the abstention rate and the false-reject spread are set by the depicted-tilt
reader rather than by the physics. We fine-tune GeoCalib on \num{5007} mocap-labelled TII frames
and \num{1097} UZH forward-rig frames, with leave-one-flight and leave-one-sequence-out folds:
each frame's label is the world-up direction in the camera frame, built from the truth attitude
and the published mount, so no pixel estimate enters a label. Scored against those labels before
any fine-tuning, the frozen reader reproduces its published error (median \SI{3.97}{deg},
\SI{90}{\percent}-trimmed RMSE \SI{4.81}{deg}, \SI{12.7}{\percent} of frames above
\SI{10}{deg}), which validates the label construction.
