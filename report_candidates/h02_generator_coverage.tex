\section{Generator coverage with path control}
\label{sec:h02-generator-coverage}

\paragraph{Scope.}
Only world models that accept a path or camera pose as control are in scope, because the
verifier asks whether the depicted attitude follows the attitude the commanded path requires.
Text-intention models and models without camera control are listed as excluded.

\paragraph{Inventory.}
Table~\ref{tab:h02-inventory} records the control parameterization, whether roll is carried,
the released weights, licence, gating and expected cost. SolarWM-Wan2.2-5B is the only
released model that both takes a camera trajectory and is practical for this pilot;
Aero-World has no public code or weights, CT-1 predicts a trajectory rather than video, and
CameraCtrl/MotionCtrl/ReCamMaster are older smaller-backbone controllers not run here.

\paragraph{SolarWM roll injection.}
SolarWM-Wan2.2-5B (Stage2, four steps, 81 frames at 480x864) accepts an absolute OpenCV
camera-to-world trajectory and rebases it to the first frame. On five probes the depicted
tilt follows the injected ramp: level 1.4\,deg, $+20^\circ$ ramp median 9.9\,deg and last-ten
22.4\,deg, $-20^\circ$ ramp 5.9\,/\,18.2\,deg with the opposite lean, triangular 18.8\,deg, and
$+60^\circ$ ramp 30.8\,/\,45.8\,deg, with lean-direction cosines 0.82--0.99. This is the first
path-controlled generator in the project whose depicted attitude follows a commanded attitude
change.

On the 75-clip designed grid the response is weaker: only cruise has a flatness-arm tilt at
least half the injected magnitude; turn-10 and turn-20 hold 13--21\,deg and the reader sees
3.8--7.1\,deg. The pre-registered roll criterion fails on tilt magnitude (1/5 paths) and
mirrored sign (1/5). The frozen verdict on the 25 flatness clips leaves 15 testable, 5 feasible
(all slalom-10) and rejects every testable turn-10/20 clip (residuals 9.1 and 14.1\,deg).
SolarWM follows the derivative of the camera path, not the held attitude the path requires.

\paragraph{Seed variance.}
The depicted tilt varies far more than ANWM's (median seed
standard deviation 1.41\,deg, median range 2.61\,deg, worst 9.88\,deg), but the oracle
best-of-3 reduction is only 1.02\,deg median and one of ten paths flips from infeasible to
feasible, below the pre-registered 2\,deg / 20\,\% criterion.

\paragraph{Full-precision LingBot.}
The bf16 fast model (five probes, 45 frames, 4 steps, 55.2\,GB, 51.7--62.9\,s/clip) reads 4.56,
3.87, 4.91, 4.29 and 4.06\,deg for level, $+20$, $-20$, triangular and $+60$ ramps, against
NF4's 3.21, 3.30, 3.77, 3.93 and 4.05. The pre-registered reader still sees no coherent roll
response; the bf16 frames look more oblique than NF4, but vertical-edge checks are not usable
on this imagery. Quantisation is therefore not established as the cause of H01's failure.
The reader/eye discrepancy is recorded, not resolved.

\paragraph{Cost and go/no-go.}
SolarWM costs 27.8\,s/clip at 480$\times$864 and four steps (about US$3.9 for 200 clips), the
cheapest path-controlled option measured; LingBot-fast bf16 costs 62.9\,s/clip (US$8.8 for
200). SolarWM is a conditional no-go for sustained commanded paths: it follows ramps but not a
held bank, and the verifier rejects the sustained turn-10/20 clips. A useful next step is a
campaign of continuously time-varying commanded attitudes, the regime where the model works.
